# Traceable Document Improvement

Improve the document without laundering uncertainty into certainty.

## Preserve first

Lock verified facts, numbers, units, terms, named roles, decision basis, and existing conclusion before rewriting. Keep unknown facts as explicit placeholders or pending items. Do not broaden authority, imply completed work, or change a proposal into an approved action.

## Improve in dimensions

- accuracy and internal consistency;
- evidence sufficiency and citation placement;
- logical completeness and execution feasibility;
- structure, information order, and cross-references;
- concise, natural, formal expression;
- tables, numbering, units, and visible formatting anomalies.

## Output modes

For recommendations, show original location, issue, proposed change, reason, and impact. For a revised draft, provide the usable text plus a compact change log linking material edits to findings or user instructions.

Writing optimization should be light and controlled: remove empty slogans, noun stacking, vague attribution, mechanical repetition, and chat residue. Do not add emotion, claims, first-person voice, facts, or invented institutional language.

Do not mark a draft as adopted. Adoption requires an explicit user decision, and any changed fact or source scope requires renewed verification.
