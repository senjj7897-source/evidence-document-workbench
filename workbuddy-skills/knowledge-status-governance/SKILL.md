# Knowledge Status Governance

Make retrieval useful without pretending that indexing proves authority.

## Independent status axes

Record separately:

- temporal status: current candidate, historical candidate, repealed with evidence, not applicable, or unverified;
- finalization status: formal evidence, formal candidate, draft, not applicable, or unknown;
- material role: policy, policy attachment, meeting proposal, report, workpaper, audit evidence, reference, style sample, or archive container;
- organizational scope and subject scope;
- source path, immutable hash, version family, and relationship evidence;
- extraction quality: searchable, partial, OCR required, spreadsheet deferred, or inaccessible.

## Evidence rules

Do not infer current legal or institutional effect from filename, directory, date, or apparent recency. Confirm current, superseded, or repealed status only from authoritative registers, issuance text, effective clauses, repeal clauses, or a verified version chain.

Text extraction and generated Markdown improve search; they do not prove signatures, formulas, hidden areas, revisions, comments, page layout, finalization, or legal effect. Return to the original only when an ambiguity can change classification or conclusion.

## Separation

Keep case facts, institutional rules, external references, and writing samples in distinct collections. Adoption of a phrase does not prove a business fact. A report's claim does not automatically become a general rule.

## Output

Produce a status manifest, version relationships, unresolved-status queue, and retrieval filters. Preserve unknown states rather than forcing a clean classification.
